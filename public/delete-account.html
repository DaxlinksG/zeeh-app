How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your zeehfi account and associated data, send an email to our support team:

  1. Email zeehafricah@gmail.com with the subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  2. Include the email address registered to your zeehfi account
  3. We will verify your identity and confirm the deletion within 5 business days
Request Deletion via Email

What Data Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your profile and account settings
  • Saved beneficiaries and preferences
  • KYC verification data (identity documents and selfie)

What Data Is Retained

Certain data must be retained to comply with financial regulations (FINTRAC, CBN, AML/CFT laws):

  • Transaction records — retained for a minimum of 5 years as required by Canadian anti-money laundering law
  • Regulatory reports — any reports filed with FINTRAC or other authorities cannot be deleted
⚠️ Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Any remaining wallet balance must be withdrawn before requesting deletion.
